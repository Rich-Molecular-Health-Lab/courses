Description

Healthy soils absorb water and carbon dioxide. But when we damage or destroy soil with deforestation or industrial farming, the exact opposite occurs: the soil releases water and carbon dioxide.

This ‘perfect storm’ of negative impact leads ultimately to desertification…but despite how hopeless that problem is, there is a way we can heal the soil and prevent further damage.

For agricultural lands that are stewarded with regenerative practices: the soil disturbance is limited, the crops and plants are diverse, and the soil is high in organic matter.

Our next film ‘Common Ground’ goes into these solutions in more depth, and is coming to theaters in September. Stay tuned for more updates and please join our movement of regeneration. http://commongroundfilm.org
